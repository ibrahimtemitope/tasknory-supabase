The Challenge

Visitors Were Leaving. Leads Were Dying.

Proxy Coding School had traffic, but no system to capture interest. Every unanswered question was a lost enrollment.

  • Website visitors browsed courses but had no easy way to ask questions
  • Interested students sent DMs or emails that went unanswered for hours
  • The team was manually replying to the same "How do I enroll?" questions daily
  • No system to collect names, emails, or course interests automatically
Before: No automated capture — just a static contact form
The Solution

AI Chatbot. Same Website. Zero Friction.

We integrated a custom AI chatbot directly into their existing site. No rebuild. No new platform. Just smart automation added to what already worked.

After: AI chatbot widget live on the homepage
  • Instant Answers: AI responds to course, pricing, and schedule questions automatically
  • Lead Capture: Collects visitor name, email, and course interest before the conversation ends
  • Demo Booking: Students can schedule a call directly through the chat interface
  • Mobile Ready: Works perfectly on phones — where 70% of their traffic comes from
  • Existing Site: Embedded into their current website without any redesign
