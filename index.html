Real Questions

Questions from Real People

The exact questions prospective clients ask us. Answered with radical honesty.

What does "verified" actually mean?
Every specialist on Tasknory passes through a 4-layer verification: (1) Government ID upload with manual admin review, (2) Portfolio review of at least 2 shipped projects on GitHub, (3) Paid trial task reviewed by a senior engineer, and (4) Live 20-minute technical screen for our top tier. See the full vetting process
What happens when a match goes wrong?
First: our free rematch guarantee. If the first specialist underdelivers, we match you with a new one within 48 hours at zero extra cost. If there's a dispute over payment, our human dispute officer acknowledges within 48 hours and delivers a written decision within 7 business days. Funds stay in escrow until resolved. See full protection details
Is 10% really all-in? Any hidden fees?
Yes, 10% is the complete platform fee. There are no "connects" to buy, no payment processing markups, no premium tier upsells, no hidden service charges. The only other cost is country-specific bank fees for freelancer withdrawals (e.g., ~$0.03 for Nigeria depending on amount). Use our fee calculator
How does the escrow actually work?
You fund your Tasknory wallet before hiring. When you hire a specialist, the project budget is locked in escrow — neither you nor the freelancer can touch it. For milestone projects, each milestone is funded and released independently after your approval. For single-payment projects, the full amount releases upon final approval. You always review before payment releases.
Why African talent specifically?
Africa has the world's youngest, fastest-growing tech workforce. Engineers across Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, and South Africa build on the same OpenAI, LangChain, and React ecosystems as Silicon Valley — often at more accessible rates. We focus on AI implementation (chatbots, automations, integrations) where this talent pool is particularly strong. Plus, timezone alignment with Europe and US East Coast enables real-time collaboration.
Is Tasknory a real registered company?
Yes. Tasknory LTD is a CAC-registered limited liability company in Nigeria. We're not a side project or an anonymous website. We've been featured in Techpoint Africa, Naijabreaking and African Startup Magazine, and we're building publicly with a mission to make African AI talent globally accessible. CAC Registration Number: Available on request via hello@tasknory.com.
Should I post a job or book a call?
Post a job if you know exactly what you need — your tech stack, budget, and timeline are clear. You write the post, our algorithm matches you with 3 specialists within 48 hours. It is free and you control everything.

Book a free call if you are not sure what you need yet, or if your project is complex and needs scoping. Our team will help you clarify requirements, choose the right tech stack, and estimate budget — then we hand-pick 3 specialists for you. Also free, just more hands-on.

Either way, you get 3 pre-vetted African AI specialists matched to your project. No risk, no upfront cost.
