How the fee is calculated:

When you withdraw $100, the total deducted is: $100 + $10 (10% platform fee) + bank fee. The $10 platform fee and bank fee are deducted from your wallet balance. You receive $100 in your bank account.

Other African countries (CF, TD, CG, GA, GW): XAF/XOF 1,500 flat bank fee (~$2.50) + 10% platform fee.
