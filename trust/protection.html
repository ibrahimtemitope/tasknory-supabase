How Your Funds Are Actually Protected

Milestone-based escrow, a rematch policy, and human support. Here's exactly what happens at each stage — and what the limits are.

How Escrow Works

The Escrow Flow

Your funds are held in your Tasknory wallet until you approve the work. The freelancer only gets paid when you say so.

1

Fund Your Wallet

You deposit the project budget into your Tasknory wallet via Flutterwave before work begins.

2

Hire & Lock Funds

When you hire, the budget is locked in escrow. You can't withdraw it. The freelancer can't touch it either.

3

Milestone Delivery

Freelancer submits work per milestone. You review and either approve or request revisions.

4

Approval & Release

Upon your approval, funds release to the freelancer automatically. No delays, no manual admin step.

5

Rematch if Needed

If work fails approval, we manually rematch you with a new specialist. Original escrow transfers.

What We Actually Guarantee

Specific policies, not vague promises. Here's what you can hold us to.

Milestone Escrow

Funds are never released in lump sums. Each milestone is funded, delivered, reviewed, and paid independently. For single-payment jobs, the full amount stays locked until final approval.

  • Single-payment: full amount held until final approval
  • Milestone jobs: each milestone funded separately
  • No upfront cash to freelancer before delivery
  • Your wallet must cover the full project cost before hiring
Live

Free Rematch Policy

If the first matched specialist doesn't deliver work that meets the brief, we manually rematch you with a new specialist. No additional commission. The original escrow funds transfer to the new match.

  • First match underdelivers? We find a replacement
  • No additional commission on rematch
  • Manual rematching by our team
  • Original escrow funds transfer to new match
Live

Human Support

Have an issue? Email us at hello@tasknory.com or use the contact form in your dashboard. A real person responds to every message. We don't use chatbots for support tickets.

  • Email: hello@tasknory.com
  • Contact form in dashboard
  • We respond to every inquiry
  • Dedicated support for active projects

Dispute Resolution

Right now, disputes are handled by our human support team on a case-by-case basis. A structured dispute resolution system is being built. For now, contact us and we'll handle it personally.

  • Contact us via platform or email
  • Human support team handles every case
