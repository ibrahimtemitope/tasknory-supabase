How We Actually Verify Specialists

Here's exactly what we check, what we don't, and where the gaps are.

Current Process

What We Check Today

Every specialist goes through these steps before they can be matched to a job.

01

Identity Verification

Specialist uploads a government-issued ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID). A human admin reviews it manually. If the document is unclear or suspicious, we reject it and ask for a clearer photo.

  • Manual review by admin team
  • Rejection with specific reason if unclear
  • Re-verification required every 12 months
Live
02

Portfolio Review

We review at least 2 shipped projects. For developers, this means GitHub repos or live links. For non-technical roles, we check work samples, case studies, or client references. We look for evidence they can deliver, not just list skills.

  • Minimum 2 projects reviewed
  • Code quality check for technical roles
  • Live demo verification where possible
Live
03

Paid Trial Task

Specialists complete a small, paid task in their domain (e.g., build a simple AI chatbot integration). A senior reviewer evaluates the output against a rubric. This is the strongest signal of actual ability — which is why we're rolling it out gradually.

  • Task is compensated even if failed
  • Human review, not automated
  • Pass/fail with written feedback
Gradual Rollout
04

Live Technical Screen

A 20-minute video call with a senior engineer. We assess communication, problem-solving under pressure, and depth of knowledge. Scored on a 5-point rubric. Only our top-tier specialists go through this.

  • Live coding or architecture discussion
  • English proficiency check
  • 5-point scored rubric
Gradual Rollout
